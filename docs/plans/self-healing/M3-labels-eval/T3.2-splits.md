# T3.2 - Leak-free splits: fresh rolling holdout, frozen golden (with review), general

## Milestone
M3. **Covers:** AC-8, G-10. **Surface:** backend.

## Branch
`healing/m3-labels-eval`.

## Objective
Build three test sets, plus a train set that shares **no split-cluster** with any of them. A split-cluster is a connected component of near-duplicate `reference_text`.

| Set | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Rolling holdout** | Sessions **after the parent version's snapshot cut-off** (v0: the last 14 days, sliding at each build: D-40). Within them, clips sampled **at random** and **verified** by you. No compared model trained on them, and they weren't chosen because the current model erred (challenge findings 9 and 11). |
| **Frozen golden** | 200 verified clips, stratified across apps and months. Frozen once it reaches 200, pinned by manifest SHA-256, never changed. It gates as non-inferiority (D-12). |
| **General** | T0.3's LibriSpeech and AMI manifest. |
| **Edited-span diagnostic** | The rolling sessions' **strong** clips, scored only at the edited positions (T3.3). It reports but doesn't gate WER. |

## Context
- A random 80/20 split leaks, because you repeat phrases (the brief).
- Clusters built on `raw_text` with a duration condition (T1.3, for storage) still leak (challenge finding 10):
  - misrecognitions differ ("kip" and "kit" land in different clusters);
  - the same sentence said slowly fails the ±30% duration gate;
  - representative assignment isn't transitive.

  So splits use **union-find components** on `reference_text` similarity, with no duration condition.

## Domain model
Source: inferred.

### Split-clusters (no `clips` column)
The union-find root of the clip's component (Jaccard ≥ 0.8 on token 3-grams of normalized `reference_text` and `raw_text`, the general set's references included) is recomputed on every read and written to each set's `clips.jsonl` sidecar. It is not stored on `clips`.

### `splits` (new)
- **Columns:**
  - `split_id`: `rolling-<parent>-<ts>`, `golden-v1`, `general-v1`, `edited-<parent>-<ts>` or `train-<parent>-<ts>`
  - `kind`: `rolling`, `golden`, `general`, `edited` or `train`
  - `parent_version`
  - `cutoff_ms`
  - `created_at_ms`
  - `manifest_path`
  - `manifest_sha256`
  - `sidecar_sha256`: the hash of `clips.jsonl` (each WAV's hash and `edited_positions`), checked by `split verify` and `eval`
  - `frozen`
  - `n_clips`
  - `n_words`
- **Invariants:**
  - exactly one golden row ever has `frozen=1`, and its sha never changes;
  - a `train` row is a snapshot (D-41): `splits.snapshot_train` writes one, frozen, whose members are exactly the clips it returned; a build never does. Its `detail` records the model, the note and each member's texts;
  - the window is a test zone: `Zones.may_train` takes no clip of a session after the cut-off and none sharing a split-cluster with one, a registered test set or the general set; `may_test` refuses every cluster golden, the general set or a recorded snapshot holds;
  - a clip in a recorded snapshot is never sampled into a random review (`--since-cutoff` or not), golden, rolling or edited;
  - a rolling row's clips all have `timestamp_ms > cutoff_ms`, `label = verified`, and `review_strategy = random`.

### `split_members` (new)
- Columns: `split_id`, `clip_id`.

### Manifest files
- `$HEALING_HOME/sets/<split_id>/manifest.json` in the benchmark's format.
- Groups:
  - `target` for clips with expected terms; the manifest `vocabulary` holds every expected term, and each term appears in its reference;
  - `ordinary` for the rest.

  The benchmark rejects any other group and any term not in `vocabulary` (`src/transcription/benchmark.rs:391-413`).
- App, session and source live in a sidecar, `clips.jsonl`.

## API
- **`healing split build [--parent current]`:** recomputes components, the rolling set from verified post-cut-off clips, golden candidates, train, and the edited-span diagnostic. Prints sizes, words, and the excluded-train count.
- **`healing review --strategy random --since-cutoff`:** samples post-cut-off clips at random for verification (T3.1). The target is about 50 per round (D-22).
- **`healing split verify`:** exits 1 on any violation:
  - a train split-cluster intersects a test split-cluster;
  - a rolling clip predates the cut-off, or isn't randomly sampled and verified;
  - the golden hash differs from its pin;
  - a gating reference comes from a `weak` or `strong` label.
- **`healing golden review`:** fills golden from random verified clips. It freezes automatically at 200 and writes the pin (the registry once M5 lands; until then `sets/golden-v1/manifest.sha256`).

## Decisions
- D-10 (revised): sessions are cut at 10-minute gaps. Rolling uses the post-cut-off sessions, not "the newest 20% of all sessions", which would blunt drift as the dataset grows.
- D-17 and D-22: golden and rolling are filled by your random-sample reviews.
- Golden pending (under 200)? It reports n/a, and the gate skips its non-inferiority check until frozen.
- Split-clusters touching golden are excluded from rolling and from train.

## Watch out for
- **A huge component** (one phrase you say constantly) removes much training data. Report `excluded_train_clips` and warn above 30%.
- **The parent's own training snapshot** (from the registry) must not contain any rolling clip. That holds by construction (cut-off), and `split verify` asserts it.
- **A rolling set under 1,500 reference words** is too small to gate. Flag `rolling_small`; the gate refuses (D-12).
- **Timestamps:** UTC ms. Sessions are cut on gaps, so DST can't matter.
- **Golden needs audio forever.** It is protected from eviction (T1.3).
- **Vocabulary leakage:** terms mined from rolling-set corrections would leak into the Tier 1 A/B. M4 mines with `--train-only`.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-8:** on a fixture of 300 clips across 40 sessions, with repeats that differ in misrecognition and speaking rate:
  - `verify` passes;
  - rolling holds only post-cut-off, random-sampled, verified clips;
  - "send it to kip" (slow) and "send it to kit" (fast) share a split-cluster and never straddle train and test;
  - every manifest is accepted by the real `hex benchmark-transcription`;
  - after golden freezes, editing a golden clip makes `verify` fail (G-10).
