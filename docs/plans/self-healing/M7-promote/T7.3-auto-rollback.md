# T7.3 - Auto-rollback when the promoted model is significantly worse

## Milestone
M7. **Covers:** AC-14. **Surface:** backend.

## Branch
`healing/m7-promote`.

## Objective
After a promotion, `healing watch` waits until either of these:
- at least 1,500 reference words of **post-promotion, random-sampled, verified** clips have arrived;
- 14 days have passed (D-19).

Then it scores the promoted and previous models on those clips. It rolls back **only if the promoted model is significantly worse**: the one-sided 95% CI lower bound of Δ WER (promoted − previous) is above 0, or `formatting` or `streaming` regressed. Otherwise it keeps the promoted model and records the result.

This is the brief's "use the same check for automatic rollback", rebuilt as a non-inferiority test. Re-using the superiority test would roll back a genuinely better model about half the time under noise, so the system would flip-flop (challenge finding 16).

## Context
- Post-promotion `raw_text` comes from the promoted model.
- The references are verified human text, not either model's output (T3.1). This is what makes the comparison fair. "Strong" references would be partly the promoted model's own words (challenge finding 8).

## API
- **`healing watch [--dry-run]`:** run by the T1.4 launchd job after collect.
- **Output:** `waiting: 600/1500 words, day 3/14` | `kept v<n>: Δ +0.2 [−0.9, +1.3]` | `rolled back v<n> → v<m>: Δ +2.1 [+0.8, +3.4]`.

## Files to change

## Watch out for
- **The previous version's GGUF was pruned:** restore it (T5.1) before scoring.
- **You switched models by hand after the promotion:** `current` mismatch (T5.1), so `watch` does nothing and warns.
- **Oscillation:** a version rolled back once is never auto-promoted again. A new round must produce a new version.
- **No reviews arrive:** after 14 days with under 1,500 words, report `insufficient evidence, kept`. Never roll back on thin data. `healing status` nudges you to review.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-14:** in fixtures:
  - a promoted model that is 2 pt worse on 2,000 words is rolled back, with the CI logged;
  - one that is 0.3 pt worse on 600 words (inside the noise) is kept;
  - one that is better is kept;
  - with too few words it waits;
  - a version rolled back once is never re-promoted automatically.

## Guardrails and exit criteria
- **G-8:** live checks use an isolated `HEX_APPLICATION_SUPPORT_DIR`. On your real Hex, rollback runs only through the launchd job you installed.
- **X-4:** a `current` mismatch with Hex's selected model → do nothing and warn.
