# T4.1 - `healing vocab mine`: pairs seen twice → candidate terms

## Milestone
M4. **Covers:** AC-10. **Surface:** backend.

## Branch
`healing/m4-vocabulary`, base `dev`.

## Objective
From the `edits` table (T2.4), find acoustic corrections that recur, the same `raw_from → to` at least 2 times (a normalized key), and turn each into a candidate term:
- `term` = `to`, with its preferred casing (the most frequent);
- `sounds_like` = the distinct `raw_from` spellings;
- a rank score = the term's frequency in the last 30 days of dictations (in `final_text` or `reference_text`) × recency decay.

## Context
- Vocabulary entries are `{term, sounds_like[]}`. A term or spelling is at most 64 characters, with up to 16 spellings per term (`src/dictation/vocabulary.rs:19-55`).
- For one person, most of the gain is in proper nouns and jargon (the brief).

## Domain model
Source: inferred.

### `vocab_candidates` (new)
- **Columns:**
  - `term` TEXT PK (the casing as it will be applied)
  - `sounds_like` JSON
  - `pair_count` INTEGER
  - `recent_uses` INTEGER
  - `score` REAL
  - `first_seen_ms`
  - `last_seen_ms`
  - `source_split` TEXT (`train` or `all`)
- **Invariants:**
  - `pair_count ≥ 2`;
  - `len(term) ≤ 64`;
  - each spelling ≤ 64 characters;
  - at most 16 spellings, keeping the most frequent;
  - a spelling never equals the term, case-insensitively.

## API
- **`healing vocab mine [--min-pairs 2] [--window 30d] [--train-only]`:** recomputes the candidates and prints them ranked.
  - `--train-only` uses only clips outside the test clusters. It is required for the A/B (T4.3), to avoid leakage (T3.2).

## Decisions
- `min-pairs = 2` (the brief). Common words (in the top 5,000 English words) are refused as terms unless `pair_count ≥ 4`. Biasing "the" would hurt.
- Multi-word terms are allowed ("Open Code"). Hex supports them.

## Watch out for
- **Conflicting targets for one spelling** (kip→Kit ×2, kip→Kip ×2). Neither wins; log the conflict.
- **Casing variants** of one term merge into one.
- **A term over 64 characters** is skipped.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-10 (mining):** in the fixture, "kip→Kit" ×2 gives a candidate. ×1 gives none. A common word needs 4 pairs. The conflict case gives no candidate.
