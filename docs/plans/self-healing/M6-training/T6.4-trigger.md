# T6.4 - `healing train auto`: the trigger

## Milestone
M6. **Covers:** AC-12. **Surface:** backend.

## Branch
`healing/m6-training`.

## Objective
`healing train auto` starts a training round once the strong (or verified) trainable clips that are new since the parent version's snapshot add up to at least 200 minutes of speech (D-33). There is no time floor.

When they hold, it runs the round:
1. prepare;
2. remote train;
3. export;
4. eval;
5. hand to the M7 gate.

Otherwise it prints what it is waiting on.

## API
- **`healing train auto [--dry-run]`**
  - Output: `waiting: strong speech 150/200 min`, or `starting round v<n>`.
  - Without `--allow-upload` in its config, it stops after `prepare` with a message (G-3).
- **Schedule:** an optional daily launchd job, like T1.4, so a round starts within a day of crossing 200 minutes. It is not installed by default.

## Files to change
- `healing/healing/train/auto.py`, `healing/healing/cli.py`, `healing/tests/test_train_auto.py`

## Watch out for
- **Minutes** are the clips' audio durations, summed from the WAVs (or the recorded sample count), not word counts or wall-clock time.
- **"New"** means clips not in the parent's snapshot, by clip id. It is not a count difference, which eviction or relabelling could fake.
- **A failed round** doesn't reset the clock. It retries at the next schedule.
- **A round already running** (lock) → exit.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-12 (trigger):** with 150 minutes of new strong speech → waiting. With 200 minutes → starts, however recently the last round ran. Clips that are strong but in the parent snapshot don't count.

## Guardrails and exit criteria
- **G-3:** without `--allow-upload` in its config, the round stops after `prepare`. Nothing is uploaded.
- **X-6:** missing GPU credentials → the round stops after `prepare` and is reported as parked.
